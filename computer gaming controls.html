Computer gaming controls

Computer gaming controls of minecraft and roblox




Minecraft

• Move Forward: W
• Move Backward: A
• Move Right: S
• Move Left: D
• Sprint: L SHIFT
• Jump: SPACE
• Crouch: L CTRL
• Mine: LMB
• Use/Place: RMB
• Inventory: E
• Toggle Perspective: F5
• Emote: B
• Chat: ENTER
• Toggle F.O.V: Z
• Game Pause: ESC
• Drop: Q

Roblox

• Move Forward: W
• Move Backward: A
• Move Right: S
• Move Left: D
• Move Camera: RMB (Hold & Move)
• Sprint: L SHIFT (As per Game)
• Jump: SPACE
• Crouch: L CTRL/ C (As per Game)
• Toggle Perspective: MOUSE SCROLL/ I & O
• Chat: /
• Inventory: `
• Game Pause: ESC
• Use/Place: LMB
• Interact: E OR F (Press/Hold)
